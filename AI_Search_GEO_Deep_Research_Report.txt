Working definitions
How RAG and vector search actually drive the answer
When a user asks "Best health centers for seniors in Bern", the engine does not keyword-match. It runs a five-
stage pipeline:
1 Intent parsing & query fan-out
The LLM decomposes the prompt into 2–4+ sub-queries (ChatGPT: 89.6% of prompts; Google AI Mode:
5–11). E.g. "Alterszentrum Bern Bewertungen", "nursing home Bern costs", "Pflegeheim Bern Rangliste".
The winning agencies are not "doing SEO better." They engineer a cross-platform entity consensus —
structured data, Wikidata, directories, reviews, digital PR — that LLMs can trust and quote.
GEO — structuring content, entities and off-site signals so generative engines cite your pages and name
your brand inside synthesized answers. Formalized by Aggarwal et al. (Princeton / Georgia Tech / IIT Delhi,
KDD 2024): up to +40% visibility lift, led by Statistics Addition, Cite Sources and Quotation Addition.
AEO — the overlapping discipline of being the direct answer (featured snippets → AI Overviews → chat
answers). In practice the industry uses GEO/AEO interchangeably.
Share of AI Voice (SOV) — the % of tracked category prompts where your brand is mentioned or cited vs.
competitors. The GEO equivalent of rank tracking.

2.1 AI Visibility Auditing & Benchmarking
The synthetic prompt testing method — the industry standard for measuring Share of Voice:
1 Prompt library construction
50–500 conversational prompts mirroring real queries, tagged by persona, funnel stage, language and
region. Sources: keyword→prompt conversion, sales/support logs, People Also Ask, AlsoAsked, and
(enterprise) Profound's real-conversation panel.
2 Scheduled multi-engine execution
Each prompt executed on every tracked engine on a fixed cadence (Peec: once/24h per model per
country). Via official APIs (cheap, but answers differ from the consumer UI) or browser-fidelity capture
(Profound's differentiator — the real rendered experience users see).
3 Response parsing
Per answer: brand mentioned? cited (URL linked)? position in answer (first recommendation vs
footnote)? sentiment (enthusiastic / caveated / dismissed)? cited domains/URLs.
4 Metric computation
Brand coverage rate · Share of AI Voice (brand mentions ÷ total category mentions) · citation frequency
per domain/URL · composite visibility score (HeyLead's "58/100") · LLM-classified sentiment score.

Chunk-level self-containment. Each H2/H3

sections with a 1–2 sentence direct answer.
Question-based headings mirroring fan-out sub-

KEY ENGINEERING DECISIONS
· API vs browser capture: APIs cheap/stable
but differ from consumer UI; browser capture is

· Non-determinism: never report single runs;
rolling averages over ≥7 daily runs.
· Cost: 50 prompts × 4 engines × daily ≈ 6,000

parsing + presentation.
