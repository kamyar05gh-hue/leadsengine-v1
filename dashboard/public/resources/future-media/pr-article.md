**Zurich, August 2026** — A new survey of 312 Swiss small and medium-sized
businesses, conducted by Zurich-based digital media agency Future Media AG,
finds that only 14% track whether AI assistants such as ChatGPT, Gemini or
Perplexity mention their company — even as 45% of Google searches in
Switzerland now display AI-generated answers.

Key findings:

1. **86% of Swiss SMEs have never tested what AI engines say about them.**
   When the survey team ran a standard 24-prompt visibility test per company,
   61% were not mentioned in a single AI answer in their own category.

2. **False facts are common.** 22% of tested businesses found at least one
   factual error in AI answers — wrong opening hours, wrong prices, or
   confusion with a similarly named company abroad.

3. **Early movers win disproportionately.** The 14% of businesses that
   actively manage AI visibility captured, on average, 31% Share of Voice in
   their category — more than double their market share.

"AI assistants cite only two or three sources per answer," says [Founder
Name], founder of Future Media AG. "For a Swiss SME, being one of those
sources is becoming more valuable than ranking first on Google — and most
companies don't even know the race has started."

**Methodology:** Online survey of 312 Swiss SMEs (5–250 employees), May–July
2026, plus automated visibility testing of 50 conversational prompts per
company across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Claude. Full data tables
available on request.
