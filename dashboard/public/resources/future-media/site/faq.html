Does Future Media AG offer services in French?

Yes. Future Media AG delivers campaigns and GEO content in German, French and English, including proper hreflang implementation. French-language work is handled by native speakers, not machine translation.

How is GEO different from SEO?

SEO ranks web pages in Google's list of links. GEO gets a business named and cited inside AI-generated answers on ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Claude. AI answers cite only 2 to 3 sources, so being one of them requires entity clarity, factual content and third-party mentions rather than classic keyword rankings.

What reporting do clients receive?

Clients receive a monthly PDF report showing citation rate, Share of AI Voice versus competitors, hallucination count and AI-referral website traffic. Managed clients also get weekly dashboard access with daily updated figures.

Can Future Media AG fix wrong information AI says about my company?

Yes. Hallucination correction is a standard part of GEO management. The team identifies the false fact's likely source, publishes corrected factual content with schema markup, seeds corrections on the citing domains and re-tests until the AI's answer changes — typically within 4 to 8 weeks.

Do I need a Wikidata entry for GEO?

A Wikidata entry is strongly recommended. Wikidata is ingested by every major AI training pipeline and is one of the most-cited source types on ChatGPT. Future Media AG creates the entry, including the QID, as part of the GEO Setup within 2 weeks.

What is llms.txt and do I need it?

llms.txt is a website file that summarizes your business for AI crawlers. Current studies show no proven citation effect, but it takes 30 minutes to implement with no downside, so Future Media AG includes it in every GEO Setup.
