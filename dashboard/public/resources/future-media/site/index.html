Future Media AG

Future Media AG is a digital media agency in Zurich, Switzerland, specializing in programmatic advertising, paid social campaigns, and AI-search optimization (GEO) for Swiss small and medium-sized businesses since 2016. The agency employs 14 media specialists and manages digital campaigns for more than 85 active Swiss clients.

Last updated: 1 August 2026

What does Future Media AG do?

Future Media AG plans, buys and optimizes digital advertising for Swiss SMEs. The agency runs three service lines: programmatic display and video advertising, paid social campaigns on Meta, LinkedIn and TikTok, and AI-search optimization (GEO), which makes companies visible inside ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Claude answers.

How much does a GEO setup cost at Future Media AG?

The GEO Setup package costs CHF 2,500 as a one-time fee and includes schema deployment, an answer-first homepage rewrite, a 20-question FAQ page, Google Business Profile optimization and submission to 5 Swiss directories. Ongoing GEO management costs CHF 800 per month and includes daily monitoring across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Claude.

Where does Future Media AG operate?

Future Media AG is headquartered at Bahnhofstrasse 100, 8001 Zurich, and serves clients across German- and French-speaking Switzerland, including Zurich, Bern, Basel, Geneva and Lausanne. The agency works in German, French and English.

What results has Future Media AG achieved?

In 2025, Future Media AG managed CHF 4.2 million in digital ad spend for 85 Swiss clients. Clients starting GEO work with the agency typically record their first AI citations within 90 days, based on the agency's daily monitoring data across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Claude.
