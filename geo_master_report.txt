1. What is GEO?
GEO = Getting AI engines to mention and cite your business in their answers.
Traditional SEO = rank #1 on Google blue links.
GEO = appear inside ChatGPT's answer when someone asks "best plumber in Zurich."
AI answers cite only 2-3 sources. Being one of them is worth more than ranking #1 on
Google.
How it works (simple version):
User asks a question →  AI searches the web →  ﬁnds chunks of text →  picks the best ones
→  builds an answer →  cites sources.
GEO makes sure your text chunks are the ones that get picked.
Key numbers:
45% of Google searches now show AI answers.
Only 12% of AI-cited pages rank in Google's top 10.
Adding statistics to your page increases AI citations by 30-40%.

3.2 The 7 Things Agencies Actually Do
Every GEO agency, whether HeyLead or Profound, does these 7 things:
1. Entity Setup — Create a Wikidata entry, Google Knowledge Graph presence, and
Organization schema with sameAs links.
2. Structured Data — Add FAQPage, LocalBusiness, HowTo schema. FAQPage alone
increases AI citations by 3.2x.
3. Answer-First Content — Rewrite pages to lead with facts, not ﬂuﬀ. Every paragraph
must contain a citable fact.
4. Citation Seeding — Get brand mentions on Reddit, Quora, industry blogs, directories,
and news sites.
5. Digital PR — Publish original research, guest posts, and expert commentary to earn
authoritative mentions.
6. Crawler Management — Conﬁgure robots.txt for 10 AI bots, deploy llms.txt, monitor bot
visits.
7. Monitoring — Track citations across 4 engines daily, measure Share of Voice, ﬂag
hallucinations.

5. Swiss Healthcare Case Study
Goal: Make "Alterszentrum Bern-Mittelland" the top citation when someone asks "Bestes
Alterszentrum fuer Senioren in Bern" on ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, or Claude.
What We Do (12 Steps)
1. Entity Home: Build an "About" page opening with: "Alterszentrum Bern-Mittelland is a
non-proﬁt senior care facility in Bern, Switzerland, providing 24-hour nursing care,
dementia support, and rehabilitation for 120 residents since 1987."
2. Wikidata: Create entry with QID, name (DE + FR), founding date, address, website,
LinkedIn, Curaviva proﬁle.
3. Schema Markup: Add Organization + LocalBusiness + FAQPage + HowTo schema.
Validate with Google's Rich Results Test.
4. Google Business Proﬁle: Complete every ﬁeld. Add 12 photos. Post weekly updates.
Respond to all reviews within 24h.
5. Directories: List on Curaviva, local.ch, search.ch, guidle.com, medregom.admin.ch.
6. Content Rewrite: Rewrite services page with answer-ﬁrst structure, staﬀ-to-resident
ratios, insurance details, speciﬁc equipment.
7. FAQ Page: 20 questions organized by: Services, Costs, Admission, Dementia Care,
Visiting. Add FAQPage schema.
8. Multilingual: Create French versions of all key pages. Add hreﬂang tags.
9. Digital PR: Publish research: "State of Dementia Care in Canton Bern: 2026 Survey." Pitch
to Berner Zeitung.
10. Community: Answer questions on r/Switzerland, Quora (senior care topics). Mention
facility name in context.
11. Monitoring: Daily prompt testing in DE/FR/EN across 4 engines. Track citation rate,
position, sentiment.
12. Refresh: Quarterly content updates. Monthly directory checks. Weekly competitor
tracking.

Key Takeaways
GEO is not SEO. SEO ranks pages. GEO gets you cited inside AI answers.
Start with entities. Wikidata + Schema.org + sameAs links are the foundation.
Facts win. Statistics, dates, speciﬁc numbers increase citations by 30-40%.
Every engine is diﬀerent. ChatGPT likes Wikipedia. Perplexity likes Reddit. Optimize for
all four.
90 days is enough. Foundation (30d) →  Content (30d) →  Monitor (30d). Leads ﬂow by
day 90-150.
